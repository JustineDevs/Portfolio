# Client Pricing Rate Card

**Effective:** September 2026  
**Scope:** All projects (B2B + small business)  
**Focus:** Full‑stack web apps, AI agents, Web3 integrations, developer tooling

---

## Hourly Rate

- **Standard hourly rate:** **$60–$90 USD/hour**

Use hourly for:
- Discovery, audits, architecture reviews
- Integrations with unclear or evolving scope
- Ongoing support outside a retainer

Guideline:
- **Small local business / tight budget:** $60–$70/h
- **B2B / funded startup / foreign client:** $75–$90/h (or higher for high‑impact work)

---

## Fixed‑Price Project Tiers

All tiers include: design + build + deploy + basic documentation + 2 weeks of post‑launch support.

### 1. Starter  
**Typical scope:**
- 1–3 core features
- Basic auth (email/password or simple OAuth)
- Simple database schema
- Basic UI (no heavy custom design)
- Deploy to a single environment (e.g., Vercel, Cloudflare, Render)

**Price range:** **$1,500–$4,000 USD**  
**Timeline:** ~1–3 weeks  

Best for:
- Small business websites with custom functionality
- Simple internal tools or dashboards
- MVPs to validate an idea quickly

---

### 2. Growth  
**Typical scope:**
- 3–7 core features / workflows
- Auth + roles/permissions
- Integrations (payments, email, 1–2 external APIs, or a basic AI agent)
- Better UI/UX, responsive, some customization
- Basic logging/monitoring, environment configs
- Deploy + simple handover docs

**Price range:** **$4,000–$12,000 USD**  
**Timeline:** ~3–8 weeks  

Best for:
- Core business applications (CRM, ops tools, customer portals)
- Products that will be used daily by staff or customers
- Startups building their first production system

---

### 3. Pro / Platform  
**Typical scope:**
- Complex workflows, multi‑step automations, or AI agents
- Multiple integrations (payments, CRM, data providers, on‑chain interactions, etc.)
- Role‑based access, audit logs, admin dashboards
- Testing, error tracking, basic CI/CD
- More robust documentation, optional training calls

**Price range:** **$12,000–$30,000+ USD**  
**Timeline:** ~6–12+ weeks  

Best for:
- Advanced B2B platforms
- AI‑heavy systems (multi‑agent workflows, evals, monitoring)
- Web3 products (wallet flows, on‑chain data, smart contract interactions)
- Systems central to revenue or operations

---

## Retainer Options (Ongoing Support)

Retainers cover: bug fixes, small improvements, monitoring, deployments, and light feature work.

- **Light support:** up to **10 hours/month** – **$800–$1,200/month**
- **Standard support:** up to **20 hours/month** – **$1,500–$2,500/month**
- **Heavy / part‑time CTO:** up to **40 hours/month** – **$3,000–$6,000/month**

Notes:
- Unused hours do not roll over (unless explicitly agreed).
- Work beyond the retainer is billed at the standard hourly rate.

---

## How to Use This Rate Card

1. **Identify the tier** that best matches the project scope.
2. **Adjust within the range** based on:
   - Client type (small business vs. funded B2B)
   - Complexity and risk (security, data, compliance, blockchain)
   - Strategic value (portfolio piece, case study, referral potential)
3. **Present pricing** as:
   - A recommended tier + range, or
   - A fixed price derived from the tier with a clear scope document.

Example phrasing for proposals:

> “Based on the scope discussed, this project fits the **Growth** tier, which typically ranges from **$4,000–$12,000 USD**. For your specific requirements, I recommend a fixed price of **$X,XXX USD**, covering [brief scope summary], with a timeline of approximately **X–Y weeks**.”

---

## Payment Terms (Suggested)

- **50% upfront**, **50% on delivery** for fixed‑price projects  
- For larger projects ($10k+): **40% upfront, 40% mid‑project, 20% on delivery**  
- Retainers: **100% upfront each month** before work begins  
- All payments in **USD** via bank transfer, Wise, or crypto (if agreed)
